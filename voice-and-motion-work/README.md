# Voice and Motion Work

Watch both pieces in the browser: **https://osawarujebril.github.io/portfolio/voice-and-motion-work/**

- **GSM story reel** (76 s, `gsm-story-reel.mp4`): a narrated story ad for my book. The voice is ElevenLabs Eleven v4, generated through the API with my own command-line tool. Every take was transcribed back and checked word by word, and the final mix sits at -14 LUFS. The AI-generated panels are animated and timed to the voice in code (HyperFrames).
- **RISE AGAIN** (10 s, `rise-again.mp4`): a short motion piece where every frame and every sound is written in code.
