GSM story reel (76 seconds)

A narrated story ad for my book. It starts from a 1989 Nigerian newspaper headline, "Telephone is not for the poor", and asks who really bought the phones when GSM arrived.

  • Voice: ElevenLabs Eleven v4, generated through the API with my own command-line tool. I picked the narrator from auditions and confirmed the voice ID through the API.
  • Checked word by word: every take was transcribed back. A take that dropped or repeated words was rejected, and a hard local name was respelled until it was read correctly.
  • Sound: voice, effects and music mixed to -14 LUFS.
  • Pictures and motion: AI-generated panels, animated and timed to the voice in code (HyperFrames).
