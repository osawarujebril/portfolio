Self-directed practice

The operating loop

Every stage has a job. Skipping one is how a plausible answer turns into a fragile system.

01

Diagnose

Find the recurring question, friction or failure before choosing a tool.

02

Design the workflow

Turn the problem into a clear sequence, decision point and useful next action.

03

Preserve context

Keep source truth, decisions and open work available through a knowledge archive.

04

Verify

Use fresh-read and verify-first controls rather than trusting a stale or merely plausible output.

05

Learn from failure

Capture the mistake, cause, correction and guardrail in a Mistake Ledger.

06

Hand off

Deliver a page, workflow, document, dashboard or decision that another person can use.
